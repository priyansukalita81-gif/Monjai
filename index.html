About us

Mon Jai Campsite & Travel

Mon Jai Campsite sits in Kisama Village, the home of the Hornbill Festival. We bring together comfortable camping, good music and warm company, and we plan travel that fits you rather than a fixed brochure.

Every booking is personal: tell us your dates, group and plans, and we customise the stay for you.
